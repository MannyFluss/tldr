# TL;DR

A five-minute game about nuance. You are a nuanced comment, and the internet
shoots words off you.

**Play it: https://mannyfluss.github.io/tldr/** (desktop or a sideways phone)

This game is tagged **[AI]**. It was made with AI (Claude), in Godot, on
purpose, and it opens by asking whether you want to see it. If you pick "hide",
you never see it, and nobody had to ban anything or lie about how it was made.
That's the argument.

- Every word of your comment is a hitbox. Downvotes knock words off; strawmen
  twist them into the opposite; screenshots crop them out of context.
- You throw [AI] tags on your own. Tagged takes self-select and leave happy.
- STATS throws real numbers from the Godot Community Poll 2026 (public results,
  11,195 responses). They work on the hitbox. They never work on the person.
- At 5:00 the thread gets locked. It always gets locked.
